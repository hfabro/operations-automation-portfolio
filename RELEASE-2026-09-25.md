# Implemented-systems recreation release

## Scope

The portfolio now leads with a Manufacturing Operations Digital Platform and eight primary implemented-system recreations: Plant Operations Hub, Facility Leak Management, Ladder Inspection, Supply Kanban, Toolbox Talks, EHS Compliance Control, Electrical Usage Analytics, and Forklift Fleet & Battery Decision Support. A simple service-intake form supports the portal.

The original CMMS, connected-operations, and Smart Factory demonstrations remain architecture/strategy evidence, not newly claimed deployments. Their operating logic was preserved. Existing case-study substance and public contact preferences were retained.

## Presentation and navigation

- Main navigation: Work, Systems, Smart Factory, About, Resume, Contact. How I Work remains a secondary route.
- Removed redundant homepage route cards; added the connected operating-platform explanation and direct hub entry.
- Source-platform-specific interfaces: SharePoint-style bands/cards, canvas-style asset workflows, simple Forms-style intake, and multi-page analytical reports.
- Recreated systems intentionally retain light operational/report surfaces independently of the dark/light portfolio shell.
- Enlarged small navigation and card text; corrected unstyled homepage demo cards and reduced excessive section spacing.
- Telemetry scale normalized to the user-supplied 69,000+ figure; no new achieved savings are attributed to simulations.

## Privacy and data boundaries

All new public visuals are code-native. The campus diagram is invented, not traced. Synthetic identifiers, people, records, and interval data are independent of the private references. Original screenshots were not copied into the release. No production URL, tenant connection, authentication, upload, tracking, or equipment command was added.

Private references remain ignored and untracked. Unrelated LinkedIn thumbnail variants were not edited or staged. One-time implementation helpers were removed; supported page assembly, local preview, validation, and fixture tests remain in tools.

## Deliberate simulation limits

- No actual SharePoint, Power Apps, Forms, or Power BI services are embedded.
- Fixed fictional dates and bounded sample sizes make scenarios repeatable. Toolbox-talk learning is illustrative and is not valid safety training or equipment authorization.
- Photo/evidence controls reference placeholders; they do not upload files.
- Report refresh is manually simulated; session state resets on reload. No cross-user persistence is claimed.
- Electrical comparison and full-bill results remain unavailable where fixture coverage is absent.
- Forklift charging assumes a stated battery capacity and simplified load window; upper-bound economics are not achieved savings or pilot approval.
- Calendar rendering is a supporting planning illustration, not a full scheduling application.
- Private reference fidelity covers workflow and hierarchy, not a pixel-for-pixel clone or copied Microsoft global chrome.

Deployment status must be checked against the final release SHA in GitHub Actions and Pages; local checks alone do not establish live deployment success.
