## Scope and decisions

This increment starts at PR #2 commit `4b805d07dbfa64136a87643118474eb64f914250` on the isolated branch `improve/executive-work-gallery`. It targets the Work gallery and its homepage entry link. PR #1 and PR #2 remain separate, unchanged review checkpoints. No merge or deployment is authorized.

The earlier gallery gave seven cases similar text-heavy cards and placed the new facilities narratives after older projects. The revised layout leads with Facilities Digital Operations, followed by financial/energy/capital decision support and bilingual training. All seven cases now have an operating question, an evidence summary, and a distinct HTML/CSS workflow schematic. These illustrations explain the logic; they are explicitly labeled as illustrative rather than live data or employer screenshots. All previous gallery destinations remain available.

Operational-focus filtering complements maturity and text search. Its `focus` query parameter supports shareable links, reload, and back navigation. The categories are editorial navigation, not deployment or outcome claims. Invalid values fall back to all work. With JavaScript unavailable, all 13 entries and their native links remain accessible. The gallery stylesheet is page-specific; existing demo and case-study implementations are unchanged. No dependencies were added to the published site.

The existing private evidence matrix informs prominence and framing only: portal and workflow coordination, decision-evidence classifications, bilingual completion controls, and established inspection/compliance/telemetry/replenishment cases. This increment introduces no new financial amounts, employer records, source exports, deployment claims, or authorship claims. Program-calculated energy, estimated costs, modeled opportunities, and realized outcomes retain their prior distinctions. The résumé is unchanged; its known extraction-order limitation remains nonblocking and outside scope.
