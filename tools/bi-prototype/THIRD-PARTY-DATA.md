# Third-party research data

The historical P2P analysis uses BPI Challenge 2019 version 1:

Boudewijn van Dongen (2019), BPI Challenge 2019, 4TU.ResearchData.
https://doi.org/10.4121/uuid:d06aff4b-79f0-45e6-8ec8-e19730c248f1

Dataset license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source-derived content includes `preview/p2p-data.js` and the historical counts/profile/schema under `curated/`. It has been transformed into counts, quality indicators, defined cohort durations, and short deterministic illustrative timelines. Original monetary values are omitted. Source identifiers and amounts were anonymized by the publisher. Attribution does not imply endorsement.

These source-derived data retain their CC BY 4.0 terms. The repository's restrictions on original portfolio code, writing, and design do not restrict rights granted by this third-party data license. The complete raw XES file is excluded from the review package. Its checksum and attribution are preserved in `source/manifest.json`.

All ERP, guided-entry, invoice-intake and connected-journey financial fixtures are independently fictional and are not attributed to this real dataset. Their code is subject to the repository's original-code terms.
