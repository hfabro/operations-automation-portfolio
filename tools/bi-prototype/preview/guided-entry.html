    How assignments are selected and preserved

    Only item ID, work-request ID, and quantity are accepted. The catalog supplies supplier, category, unit, and price. The work request supplies the site, asset, work order, and project. Exactly one effective approved finance rule must supply a valid account and cost-center distribution totaling 100%.

    Unmapped requests, conflicting rules, unapproved suppliers, closed projects, and invalid distributions are held. No nearest-match or guessed account is substituted. Each staged record retains its inputs, coding, policy version, effective dates, and owner. Later master-data changes do not rewrite an existing staged record. Retrying the same submission cannot create a second request.

    What is still a demonstration

    The rule evaluation date is fixed at November 15, 2026 for reproducibility. Master data and approvals are fictional. Session role switches elsewhere do not authenticate anyone. Persistent master-data governance, change approval, real ERP validation, requisition approval, budget checks, and automatic PO conversion require further implementation. A browser demonstration cannot prevent an administrator from altering its source.

    Other repeatable controls can use the same pattern: required evidence before completion, consistent units and identifiers, duplicate prevention, controlled status transitions, and reporting-quality checks.
