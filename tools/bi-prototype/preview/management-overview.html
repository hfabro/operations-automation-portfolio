Management figures need a defined meaning.

One snapshot, separate grains and accountable decisions

The financial position reuses the same purchasing journal and integer-cent analytical projection as the detailed report. Owner queues count distinct held invoice documents. Coding holds count requisitions separately. Coding coverage uses only approved PO lines as its denominator; a 100% approved-line result does not mean every request has an approved mapping.

Recorded work is compared with its captured purchasing and maintenance command histories. Retained earlier-context work remains visible and does not complete a replacement scenario. Payment and normal readings alone do not prove completed maintenance.

Fictional timestamps do not establish business performance

The example includes four approved PO lines across three cost centers, a paid pump repair, posted unpaid filter cost, invoice receipt/price holds, one unresolved coding request and work awaiting independent verification. All records and outcomes are fictional. Source action and completion timestamps are displayed as evidence, not used to invent aging, trend, cycle-time improvement or savings metrics.
