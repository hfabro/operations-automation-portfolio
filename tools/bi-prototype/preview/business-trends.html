What the dates and financial measures mean

The declared cohort is SYN-BUSINESS-TIME-1 / SYN-CO-01. Source events have explicit occurrence, recording and Chicago business dates. A cutoff includes that complete business date; period flows begin on the chosen start date. Opening balances include only earlier events. The complete source is validated even when later events are excluded from the chosen cutoff.

Financial activity uses event × PO line × cost-center allocations; document counts use distinct document IDs. USD amounts use integer cents. Original invoice dates and fictional Finance-owned calendar net-30/net-45 terms establish due dates where the source permits. Missing dates stay unknown. Generated core replay timestamps are diagnostic and do not determine business aging.

How this showcase is built and what it demonstrates

HTML, CSS and JavaScript provide the interface. A local browser worker replays the same governed purchasing, invoice, machine and work rules used by the connected demonstrations. A deterministic authored fixture supplies the 90-day cohort. Date controls query that model; bars, tables and downloads expose its reconciled outputs. No external service, saved-journal replacement or production credentials are needed.

This is a browser BI demonstration, not a native Power BI report, OCR service or production authorization system. The first version excludes tax, credits, FX conversion, partial settlement, backdating, delayed-day capture and full GL/period close. Fictional patterns do not establish measured savings, OEE, operating improvement or avoided failures.
