Why the same vendor can have different coding

Supplier identity identifies who sent the invoice. The approved PO line supplies the business purpose, account, project, asset, work order, and cost distributions. In the sample, the same pump supplier invoices an equipment upgrade and a routine repair; each retains the appropriate approved PO coding. An absent PO does not trigger a vendor-based account guess.

How automatic sorting works

The batch uses a fixed fictional ERP and supplier snapshot. Exact approved supplier IDs or unique registered aliases resolve identity. Conflicts are held. Checks cover required metadata, entity/currency, PO ownership and coding, posted invoice references, accepted uninvoiced quantity, and the demonstration's inclusive 1% price tolerance.

All occurrences of a repeated document ID or supplier invoice reference are held. If otherwise eligible invoices collectively exceed a PO line's available accepted quantity, all competing candidates are held. Input order does not decide which invoice wins. Existing records and financial balances are unchanged.

Boundaries and next integration steps

This slice supports one fictional entity, USD net values, whole-unit quantities, and one PO line per invoice. It does not yet handle taxes, freight, FX, credits, full multi-line document matching, fuzzy duplicate detection, extraction confidence, dates/due-date prioritization, or document retention. PDF/email capture, verified extraction, persistent queues, resubmission, controlled posting, and real ERP validation are separate integration steps. Review-queue ownership is illustrative; no user is authenticated.
