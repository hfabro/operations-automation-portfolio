What approval changes and what it preserves
The request supplies item, work-purpose and quantity only. The catalog and approved work context supply business identity. Finance explicitly owns the account and distribution; the percentages must total 100% across approved centers. Pending and rejected proposals cannot supply coding. Approval requires a different person, cannot backdate effectiveness, and reruns overlap checks against approved mappings.
The effective interval includes its start and excludes its end. The dated check uses the policy known through its decision date and records the applied rule/version. The original November 15 hold remains unchanged. This workbench adds rules to a separate snapshot; rule replacement, revocation, full master-data release integration and recoding existing ERP journals are outside this version.
How the demonstration is built
HTML/CSS/JavaScript present an append-only fictional Finance command journal. The existing pure coding resolver evaluates both the unchanged baseline and the separately approved dated snapshot. Downloads contain inputs, proposal/review evidence, policy versions, original and new results, and a replayable journal. Reload/reset clears this temporary workbench; export first to retain evidence.
Person/role controls illustrate separation of duties, not production authentication or authorization. Nothing is written to shared browser journals or employer systems. All policy choices, people, records and outcomes are fictional.
