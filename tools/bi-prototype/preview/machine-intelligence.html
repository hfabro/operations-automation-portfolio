Rule, data grains and measurement boundaries
One source observation identifies an asset, tag, UTC event time and source sequence. Conflicting IDs and ambiguous asset/tag/time records are quarantined; identical IDs are counted once. Bad and uncertain quality remain visible but cannot establish an operating state or trigger a condition. Interval boundaries are half-open: [start, end). The replay uses event time, with no future readings.
One condition case groups repeated episodes for an asset/rule/day. Maintenance decisions and purchasing commands retain their own journals. Costs join through the exact staged request ID, not through every purchase sharing an asset. Temperature is contextual only; no temperature diagnosis is implemented.
This is a browser simulation of an observation-to-business architecture, not a live SCADA, PLC, OPC UA, MQTT or historian connection. No commands are sent to equipment. No throughput, OEE, avoided failure, actual savings or enterprise authentication claim is made.
All observations, assets, reviews, transactions and outcomes are fictional. The rule and criticality are scenario assumptions. UTC capture window: 15 Nov 2026, 08:00–08:30. Source and exclusion counts describe the full capture; operational measures and conditions follow the replay position. Historical public-data analysis is a separate demonstration.
