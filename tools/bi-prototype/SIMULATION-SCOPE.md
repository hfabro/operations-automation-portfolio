# Portfolio scope: simulated professional presentation

The entire portfolio is an interactive simulated presentation for prospective employers and clients. Its purpose is to demonstrate business judgment, process design, repeatable automation, data integrity, architecture and analytical capability.

ERP transactions, suppliers, controls, approvals, machine/SCADA integrations and operational outcomes may be fictional. The software behavior, calculations and verification should work. Public research data can support real historical analysis when clearly attributed and separated from the fictional company. Simulated results must not be presented as employer achievements or realized savings.

No employer ERP access, production-machine connection, company credentials, live operational deployment, enterprise authentication or paid integration is required to complete the showcase. Such architecture can be explained or simulated. References in earlier planning notes to those items as pending integration requirements are superseded by this scope.

Success means that a visitor can understand the business problem, try a credible scenario, inspect how the automation decides, trace data/coding and see the result. New scenarios should improve that experience rather than expand into unnecessary production infrastructure.

Actual publication, repository protections, accurate attribution, working browser behavior and appropriate privacy remain real responsibilities for this publicly accessible portfolio. Native report artifacts should be labeled as such only when built and verified; HTML simulations should be labeled accurately. Native BI creation is a presentation deliverable, not a requirement to connect to a company's systems.
