## Limits and deliberately unchanged behavior

These are synthetic browser recreations, not embedded Microsoft applications or proof of tenant deployment. No real plant drawings, employer screenshots, account chrome, credentials or live connections are included. Native touch scrolling is provided by CSS; a physical phone/tablet touch test was not performed. This is not a full assistive-technology audit or a pixel-perfect clone. Existing transactional rules, interval calculations, modeled boundaries and synthetic records were preserved. Kanban uses physical reorder signals, not a newly invented live remaining-inventory count.
