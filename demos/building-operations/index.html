Architecture boundary

Visualization follows the operating model.

This concept can accept human reports and approved signals, but it intentionally separates awareness from equipment control. Commands, interlocks, and safety functions remain in qualified control systems.

01 / identity

Location and asset first

A report is anchored to a controlled building zone, map revision, or equipment record.

02 / severity

Explicit escalation

Immediate hazards direct people to secure the area and notify the accountable function.

03 / lifecycle

One owned response

Open, acknowledged, work requested, and resolved states make responsibility visible.

04 / history

Evidence survives the map

Closeout, corrective action, and trend records remain available after markers disappear.

Synthetic demonstration. No information is submitted, stored, or connected to production or building systems.
