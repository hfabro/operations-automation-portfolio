    Deterministic decision rules
    • All six responses are required.
    • Fail or Review requires notes.
    • A critical Fail or “Remove from service: Yes” creates a corrective action.
    • Exceptions create a simulated notification event.
    • The inspection occurrence is preserved separately from the asset master.

    Architecture behind the interaction

    The interface is one layer of the system.

    The proof of concept separates identity, execution, workflow, and history so the operating process can remain supportable as requirements change.

    01 / identity

    Asset master

    LAD-017 owns stable information such as location, department, class, and inspection frequency.

    02 / occurrence

    Inspection record

    Each execution gets a unique ID and captures the responses that were valid at that moment.

    03 / exception

    Corrective action

    Rules create follow-up work when unsafe conditions are reported; AI is not needed for predictable safety logic.

    04 / sustainment

    History and reporting

    Completed occurrences remain historically true and become the reporting-ready record of execution.
