Synthetic maintenance system prototype

Preserve the instruction. Control the work. Keep history true.

Generate a PM occurrence from a versioned job plan, execute it as a technician, route deterministic exceptions, and review the resulting history. The prototype demonstrates lifecycle architecture—not a deployed CMMS.

Prototype boundary Browser-only · synthetic · no writeback

No live CMMS, ERP, asset, employee, plant, or maintenance data. No autonomous work decisions. Session state is discarded when the page is refreshed.

Lifecycle architecture

A completed PM is a historical record—not a live template.

When an occurrence is generated, the applicable template revision is snapshotted. Future improvements change future work without rewriting past instructions.

    Design decisions

    The interface is downstream of the operating model.

    01

    Separate definition from execution

    Assets, plans, templates, and scheduled occurrences have different lifecycles and should not collapse into one record.

    02

    Make exceptions explicit

    Out-of-range readings, issues, blocked steps, missing evidence, and rework each need controlled paths.

    03

    Preserve accountable judgment

    Rules can route work and detect threshold breaches. People still validate findings and authorize the disposition.

    04

    Design for the system of record

    A production build would require validated ownership, integration, permissions, cybersecurity, document control, and support.
