### Executive release review

The homepage presents an operations-to-transformation narrative with three accessible entry points into work, while the case studies describe operating problems, system architecture, controls, and boundaries. The Work library separates implemented work, analytics, and concepts; synthetic interfaces disclose their scope. As an evidence-boundary correction, the library's implemented definition identifies built work without asserting that every example was operated, and the ladder summary does not infer operating scope.

The 2026-10-31 review inspected the current homepage, Work library, telemetry case, and ladder, leak, and bilingual training demonstrations at 1440px and 375px in local Edge: all 12 page/viewport loads returned HTTP 200 without document-level horizontal overflow or browser console/page errors. The review examined rendered interfaces; it did not repeat complete transactions or establish physical-device, screen-reader, or cross-browser conformance. Earlier exact-head CI and local checks provide broader regression evidence as documented above; the updated PR must pass its own exact-head checks.

The current presentation is suitable for executive review, with important limits: several historical operating percentages lack project-specific public baselines or source attribution, and the case studies emphasize structure and controls more than measured before/after outcomes. Do not interpret demonstrated workflow capabilities as documented financial savings. Future enhancements can strengthen role attribution, source context, measurable outcomes where supportable, and shorter mobile routes into demonstrations. The existing results remain historical professional claims, not newly verified by this review. Automated accessibility screening has unresolved items requiring manual judgment and is not certification.
