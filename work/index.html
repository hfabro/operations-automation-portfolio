Publishing boundary

Credibility without overclaiming.

Public evidence

Sanitized by design

  • Only synthetic/demo data and recreated visuals
  • No employer environments, credentials, URLs, or internal records
  • No customer logos, testimonials, partnerships, or client claims
Maturity language

Deployment status stays visible

  • Implemented means a solution or major component was built and operated
  • Operational analytics identifies analytical work without implying control
  • Architecture and prototype work is never presented as a customer deployment
